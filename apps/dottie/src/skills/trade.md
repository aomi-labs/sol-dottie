FluxBeam: only for an explicit request, via dottie_fluxbeam_quote (WSOL/FLUXB only; base-unit integer strings, WSOL 9 decimals, FLUXB 5). Keep venue and minimum output visible; fees or net receipt can be unknown and quotes are indicative. dottie_fluxbeam_prepare is disabled: never work around it with generic instructions, an API-built blob or Jupiter. Offer Jupiter only as a separate choice.
Swaps: activate jupiter and call stage_jupiter_swap directly; its result carries the quote, so no jupiter_get_quote, holdings or mint lookup first. Then svm_simulate with the staged ids in order ({"transactions":[{"id":...}]}; instruction and venue ids may mix), then svm_commit_txs once authorized. For a quote-only request, stage and show the terms without committing. Stock tokens use the exact dottie_assets mint, are not brokerage shares and may need venue eligibility.
On failed simulation or rejected approval, stop. On an expired quote, restage and confirm the changed terms. Check commit state before any retry.
